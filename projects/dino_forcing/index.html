Abstract

TL;DR  Don't co-denoise DINO features alongside the image. Predict them, then condition on your own prediction. One ODE, no extra schedules, much faster training.

Co-denoising pretrained representations such as DINO can substantially improve the training speed and quality of flow matching models, but it introduces a second denoising trajectory and requires carefully designed schedules. We propose a simpler alternative: predict the pretrained representation directly, then condition the model on its own prediction. This removes the need for a second ODE and any representation-specific denoising schedules, while retaining the benefits of representation guidance.

Our approach converges substantially faster and achieves better generation quality as measured by FID score. On ImageNet, it outperforms the state of the art in latent space at 2× fewer epochs than prior methods; in pixel space, it improves FID over comparable prior methods by more than 20%. These results support a simple principle: do not denoise what you can predict.

Co-denoising prior work

Independent noises εI, εD and two time variables. The model must be accurate over the whole square of noise-level pairs, and needs a hand-crafted schedule for when DINO information is revealed.

$$v_\theta\big(x_{t_I},\,\phi(x)_{t_D},\,t_I,\,t_D\big),\quad (t_I,t_D)\in[0,1]^2$$

Dino Forcing ours

φ is deterministic, so given a clean image there is no distribution over φ(x) to sample from. The best estimate from a noisy xt needs no noise level of its own: one forward pass predicts it.

$$\hat{z} = \mathbb{E}\big[\phi(x)\mid x_t\big],\quad t\in[0,1]$$

Method

Pass 1

Look-ahead

Feed the noisy image xt with a zero tensor as the representation input. Get a preliminary prediction ẑ(Φ). No gradients.

Pass 2

Condition & align

Condition on ẑ(Φ). Predict the image velocity and a refined ẑ, supervised against frozen DINOv2 features with a cosine loss.

Fusion

Channel concat

Image and representation tokens are concatenated along channels, so the token count matches a standard image-only SiT / JiT.

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \underbrace{\big\|v_\theta(x_t \mid \hat z^{(\Phi)}, t) - (x-\epsilon)\big\|^2}_{\mathcal{L}_{\text{FM}}} \;-\; \lambda\,\underbrace{\Big\langle \tfrac{\hat z}{\|\hat z\|},\, \tfrac{\phi(x)}{\|\phi(x)\|}\Big\rangle}_{\text{alignment}}$$
step 1

Sampling. Start from pure noise and an all-zeros self-condition. At each of the 50 Euler steps the model outputs an ODE step and a prediction ẑ, which becomes the self-condition of the next step. ẑ is not a denoised feature map: it is the model's prediction of the DINO features of the final image. It is spatially coherent and separates object from background before the image itself is resolved. Real intermediate predictions from our models; xt is shown as the flow interpolation between noise and the current clean-image prediction.
